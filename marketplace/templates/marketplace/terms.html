6. Platform Circumvention

Platform Circumvention includes:

  1. Exchanging contact details through the Coconut Wireless Network and then arranging work outside the Platform;
  2. Cancelling, removing, or closing a task and later engaging directly for the same or similar work;
  3. Using Platform information to avoid fees;
  4. Encouraging another user to avoid Coconut Wireless Network fees.

6.2 Platform Circumvention Fee

If the Coconut Wireless Network reasonably determines that Platform Circumvention occurred within twelve (12) months of introduction through the Platform, the Coconut Wireless Network may charge a Platform Circumvention Fee.

The Platform Circumvention Fee is:

  1. Five percent (5%) of the total value of the services performed or contracted; or
  2. FJD $50.00,

whichever is greater. The fee is payable separately by both the Client and the Provider.

6.3 Evidence and determination

The Coconut Wireless Network may rely on Platform messages, user admissions, task history, quotes, invoices, account activity, documents, or other reasonably available information when determining whether Platform Circumvention has occurred.

6.4 Enforcement

The Coconut Wireless Network may suspend accounts, restrict quoting privileges, terminate accounts, recover unpaid fees as a debt, and recover reasonable legal or collection costs where Platform Circumvention is found to have occurred.
